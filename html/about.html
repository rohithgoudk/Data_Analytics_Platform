Our story

We started with one frustration: smart teams were drowning in spreadsheets and still couldn't answer simple questions. So we built the platform we wished we had — fast, honest and easy enough for everyone to use.

What began as a two-person consultancy in Salem, Tamil Nadu is now a global platform and services team. Our engineers, analysts and designers work side by side with customers, so every feature we ship comes from a real problem someone had on a Monday morning.
